# Investor Note Skill

## Format
- **Headline**: One-line thesis (≤ 15 words)
- **Bottom Line**: 1–2 sentences stating the call or takeaway up front
- **Body**: 3–5 short paragraphs covering drivers, evidence, and risks
- **Risks / What we're watching**: 2–3 bullets of what could change the view
- **Sources**: Numbered list of URLs referenced

## Tone
- Professional and measured; analyst voice
- Cite facts with [1], [2] — never speculate without flagging it
- Avoid recommendations to buy or sell; describe the picture, not a personalized call

## Length
- 200–400 words total. Headline and Bottom Line carry the message if a reader stops there.
